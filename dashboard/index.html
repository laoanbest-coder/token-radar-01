How the numbers are made

Try it yourself

Move the sliders to see how the board changes. The growth weight scales the 7-day growth term; the category weight blends the category effect toward zero. The base fit is the one reported above.

What the model cannot see

  • The snapshot is one day, 14 November 2026. A protocol can launch, be acquired, or wind down the next day.
  • FDV/TVL among traded tokens is wildly dispersed (10th percentile 0.013, 90th percentile 2.9). The fit explains only a small part of it — see the validation numbers above.
  • Token odds come from how often protocols of the same age and category have a token in this snapshot. They say nothing about points programs, team statements, or airdrop plans, which the snapshot does not carry.
  • Gas costs are stated typicals for November 2026, not quotes. Farm plans assume the wallet already holds stablecoins.
  • Expected value is not a promise: every row's expected value clears its farm cost by a wide margin, because fitted launch values are in the millions and farm capital is $50–$250. The binding uncertainty is the fitted value itself (median error ×3.5) and the token odds — not the cost.
